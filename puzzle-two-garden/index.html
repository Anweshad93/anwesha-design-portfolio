The water keeps the way.

The traveller enters a garden where stepping stones lie scattered across a moonlit pool. Long ago, the inhabitants left a path in the water.

Step onto the first stone. Press and hold a scattered tile, then drag it onto the glowing outline in the water with the same symbol. Match both outlines to build a path.
